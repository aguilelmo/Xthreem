From frame to finished bike

Making full-suspension frames in Switzerland would put a kids' bike out of reach for most families. So we chose a frame supplier we have tested ourselves.

Everything a young rider actually feels happens here in Ticino: every part assembled by hand, suspension set for their weight, saddle and levers fitted to them on pick-up day.

Behind it is Davide, with years of workshop and bike-shop experience, who follows every bike from order to hand-over.

Who it fits
Riders of about 125–145 cm: we always check the size together

Fork, shock and drivetrain can be upgraded on request. Before you pay, you get the full spec sheet with the exact components.

How it works

  • Payment

    In full when you order, by QR-bill. Card or TWINT on request.

  • Ready in 6–8 weeks

    From the payment date. We update you at every step, with photos of the build.

  • Money-back guarantee

    If the bike isn't ready within 10 weeks, you get everything back.

  • Pick-up in Ticino

    Included, with fitting to your kid. Shipping within Switzerland about CHF 99: we confirm the exact cost with your address, before payment.

  • 2-year warranty

    On parts and assembly, plus a free check after about 2 months of riding.

  • Talk to us directly

    Any question, email info@xthreem.ch: we reply within 3 working days.

Full terms of sale

Images are graphic previews: the delivered bike may differ in cosmetic details, never in the confirmed technical spec.

The price is fixed and won't change after payment. Upgrades are agreed and paid before ordering.

Your order is confirmed once payment is received; you get a written confirmation with an order number.

You can cancel free of charge within 24 hours of payment. After that, the 10-week money-back guarantee applies.

Returns: if the bike is unused and as new within 14 days of hand-over, you can return it and we refund you within 30 days of receiving it. Used bikes can't be returned.

The warranty doesn't cover normal wear (tyres, brakes, chain), crashes or changes made by third parties.

Always ride with a helmet. Parents decide where and how their kid rides.

Your data is used only for the order, hand-over and warranty. Swiss law applies.
